Welcome to the Kapinga Community Hub

Our Mission: To connect, preserve, and empower the Kapinga community across islands and oceans by providing a central platform for cultural celebration, historical storytelling, and active community engagement.

Welcome to our official digital gathering place! The Kapinga Community Hub is dedicated to bringing our people together—whether you are living on our home island or connecting from abroad. Explore our pages to discover our history, stay connected with fellow members, and join us in honoring the vibrant spirit of ti-angai.
